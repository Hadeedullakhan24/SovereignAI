Annual statutory inspection under the Boilers Act, including hydro test, internal furnace inspection, and safety valve testing.

## 3. Findings
Boiler is in satisfactory operating condition. No structural defects identified. Routine soot cleaning recommended as a maintenance item, not a safety concern.
