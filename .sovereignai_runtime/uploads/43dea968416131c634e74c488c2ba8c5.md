# PIPELINE INSPECTION REPORT

**Report No:** INS-PL-0021
**Date of Inspection:** 12-Aug-2026
**Location:** Crude Transfer Line, Section B, Tank Farm Area
**Inspector:** J. Rao, Certified NDT Level II
**Line ID:** CT-104-B (Carbon Steel, 12" NB, Schedule 40)

## 1. Scope of Inspection
Routine annual inspection of the crude transfer pipeline section between Tank Farm Valve Station 3 and Pump House 2, including visual inspection, ultrasonic thickness (UT) survey, and external corrosion assessment.

## 2. Findings

### 2.1 Visual Inspection
- Minor surface corrosion observed at support saddle locations (approx. 15% of coating loss)
- Pipeline insulation cladding damaged at three locations (chainage 45m, 120m, 210m)
- No visible leaks, weeping, or seepage at flanged joints

### 2.2 Ultrasonic Thickness Survey
| Location (Chainage) | Nominal Thickness (mm) | Measured Thickness (mm) | Remaining Life (est.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0m (near valve station) | 9.5 | 8.9 | >15 years |
| 45m | 9.5 | 8.1 | ~9 years |
| 120m | 9.5 | 7.4 | ~5 years — monitor |
| 210m | 9.5 | 8.6 | >12 years |

### 2.3 Corrosion Assessment
Localized external corrosion at chainage 120m attributed to damaged insulation allowing moisture ingress. Corrosion rate estimated at 0.15mm/year based on historical thickness data.

## 3. Recommendations
1. Repair insulation cladding at all three identified locations within 30 days.
2. Schedule follow-up UT survey at chainage 120m within 6 months.
3. Apply protective coating to support saddle areas during next planned shutdown.
4. No immediate pressure de-rating required; line remains fit for service per OISD-141 guidelines.

## 4. Conclusion
The pipeline section is in **satisfactory condition** for continued operation, with monitoring required at chainage 120m due to accelerated localized corrosion. No immediate safety concern identified.
